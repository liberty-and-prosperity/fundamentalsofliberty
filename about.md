This website is NOT a blog. There is no intention to add new “posts” on a regular basis. ***Fundamentals of Liberty*** is meant to be a mostly stable educational resource for those who wish to begin learning about Liberty. However since this is the internet, the site will no doubt change over time. If you would like to be informed when changes are made, please use the Contact page and specifically request to be placed on the update list. I won’t send you any emails unless you ask to be on that list. Naturally, your email address will not be shared with any other person or organization.
