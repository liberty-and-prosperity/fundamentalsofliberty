## 2026-09 | whole site | Restoration from archived copies

**Sources**

- Page text: Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) copies of fundamentalsofliberty.com, 2016 to 2018. These are the last copies of the WordPress site before it went offline.
- Missing pictures: the author's own Word drafts of the essays (February 2010).
- The author's Word documents and notes stay in the family's private archive, not in this repository.

**The author's words**

- No words were changed. A computer comparison with the archived pages confirmed this.
- The only differences: the old "previous" and "next" links and tables of contents are now built automatically, and one word that the old page split in two ("publis hed") is joined.

**Formatting**

- Removed the mixed font sizes, font names, and colors of the old editor.
- Changed underlined text to italics, or removed the underline where the text was already bold. The author's own to-do list in his notes said "remove non-link underlining."
- Joined line breaks inside a few bold sentences.
- Made the numbered paragraphs on "Preamble to the Constitution" a real numbered list. Fixed the spacing in "6 ."
- Made the five book titles on "Five Books" a bulleted list.
- Shortened Amazon links. They go to the same books.
- Removed three broken screenshot placeholders from "Five Books." They were already broken on the original site.

**Pages**

- Five Books: removed the copy of Governor Mitch Daniels' interview. It is someone else's copyrighted work, and the original is online again. the author's introduction and book list stay. A family note links to the interview and to an archived copy of the original page.
- Contact: removed. The old form no longer works. The old Contact address now goes to About. A family note on About explains this.
- About: added a family note.
- Footer: the original footer said only "Fundamentals of Liberty" and "Proudly powered by WordPress." The new footer does not name the author. The family will decide whether to name him.

**Pictures**

- The pictures on "The Purpose of Government," "Our Interactions with Government," and "The Essence of Liberty" were stored on the author's Blogspot account and were not archived with the site. They were restored from his Word drafts.
- Resized the largest photos to 1200 pixels wide.
- Added a short text description to every picture for readers who use screen readers.

**Addresses**

- Pages have new, readable addresses, for example `/essays/the-purpose-of-government/`.
- Old WordPress addresses such as `/?page_id=583` still work. They go to the new pages. The list is in `_data/legacy_urls.yml`.
